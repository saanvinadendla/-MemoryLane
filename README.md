# Alibi
Create interactive timelines, uncover connections between memories, and generate meaningful year-in-review recaps that bring your life’s moments together.
Turn your everyday moments into meaningful stories.

Alibi is an AI-powered digital memory journal that transforms your photos, notes, and everyday experiences into personalized visual stories. Capture the moments that matter, rediscover forgotten memories, and see your life unfold through intelligent timelines, emotional posts, support, and beautifully generated recaps.

AI Memory Journal goes beyond traditional journaling by connecting the pieces of your life; helping you uncover patterns, revisit meaningful experiences, and preserve your personal story in a more visual and engaging way.

## 🌟 Features

### 📸 Smart Memory Capture
Upload and organize photos, notes, and everyday moments.
Preserve personal experiences in one centralized digital space.
Turn scattered memories into an organized personal archive.

### 🧠 AI-Powered Memory Connections
Automatically discover relationships between memories.
Identify recurring people, places, events, and themes.
Surface forgotten moments based on meaningful connections.
Transform individual memories into a bigger picture of your life.

### 🗓️ Interactive Memory Timeline
Explore your memories through an interactive visual timeline.
Navigate through days, months, and years.
Revisit important milestones and everyday experiences.
Discover how your story evolves over time.

### 🎨 Personalized Visual Stories
Generate AI-powered stories from your photos, notes, and experiences.
Transform collections of memories into engaging visual narratives.
Create meaningful stories around trips, events, celebrations, and milestones.

### 💔 Emotional Support Posts and Activities
Share your downs as well as receive support from others. 
A place to vent and feel seen while receiving advice.
Attend events to improve emotional wellbeing!
Buy squishies and toys that help relieve stress.

### 📅 Year-in-Review Recaps
Automatically generate personalized annual memory recaps.
Highlight memorable experiences, milestones, and recurring themes.
Visualize your year through curated stories and memorable moments.
Rediscover the experiences that made each year unique.

## 💡 Why AI Memory Journal?
Life happens in moments, but those moments are often scattered across photo galleries, notes apps, and different platforms.

AI Memory Journal brings everything together.

Instead of simply storing memories, it helps you understand, connect, and relive them. By combining artificial intelligence with visual storytelling, it creates a more meaningful way to document your personal journey.

Your memories are more than files. They're your story.
